Before you export.

This tile carries a grammar older than any one hand. Releasing it into a new body — SVG, mold, clay — is a cultural act, not a technical one.

Heritage condition —

Your tile carries a TLR (Tile Lineage Record) and per-city governance metadata embedded in the SVG. TLR is tile-engine-native and parallel to — not the same as — Vibeweaving's VDNS notation for textiles.
